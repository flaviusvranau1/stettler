## Q1. Romanian structures in 2026: PFA (sistem real) vs SRL micro vs SRL on profit tax. Rates, thresholds, costs, worked net take-home

### Takeaway
**2026 parameters:**
- The minimum gross wage is 4,050 lei from January to June 2026 and 4,325 lei from 1 July 2026 (HG 146/2026).
- The CAS/CASS thresholds for 2026 income are most likely computed on 4,050 lei, but sources conflict on this.

**PFA:**
- Income tax is 10%.
- CAS is 25%, charged on a fixed base of 12 minimum wages (48,600 lei) or 24 minimum wages (97,200 lei), and only once net income reaches 12 minimum wages.
- CASS is 10% of actual net income. As an employee he has no 6-minimum-wage floor. The cap is 72 minimum wages.

**SRL micro:**
- The tax is 1% of revenue; this single rate applies from 2026.
- The revenue cap is €100,000.
- The company needs at least one full-time-equivalent employee, or an administrator on a mandate contract paid at least the minimum wage.

**Dividends:** taxed at 16% from 2026, plus CASS in fixed tiers of 6, 12 or 24 minimum wages.

**Romanian side only, before US costs and before the SRL's higher compliance cost:**
- At 30k lei, PFA is best.
- At 60k lei, PFA and a profit-tax SRL are roughly tied, and micro is slightly worse.
- At 120k lei, micro or a profit-tax SRL beats PFA by about 4.5–6.8k lei.
- PFA has two CAS "cliff" traps, at 48,600 lei and 97,200 lei.

**PFA, real system (sistem real)**
- **Income tax.** The rate is 10% of net income, and the contributions paid reduce the taxable base. ContApp's example: net income 30,000 lei, no CAS, CASS 3,000 lei, taxable base 27,000 lei, tax 2,700 lei — [contapp taxe PFA 2026](https://contapp.ro/blog/taxe-pfa-2026/)
- **CAS 25% (pension):**
  - Net income of at least 12 minimum wages (48,600 lei): the base is 12 minimum wages, so CAS is 12,150 lei.
  - Net income of at least 24 minimum wages (97,200 lei): the base is 24 minimum wages, so CAS is 24,300 lei, the maximum regardless of income.
  - Below 12 minimum wages, CAS is optional.
  - Sources: [contapp taxe PFA 2026](https://contapp.ro/blog/taxe-pfa-2026/); [contapp plafoane 2026](https://contapp.ro/blog/plafoane-fiscale-2026-pentru-pfa-ii-si-microintreprinderi/)
- **CASS 10% (health):**
  - CASS is 10% of net income. The base runs from 6 to 72 minimum wages (24,300–291,600 lei), so the maximum CASS is 29,160 lei — [contapp taxe PFA 2026](https://contapp.ro/blog/taxe-pfa-2026/)
  - The cap rose from 60 to 72 minimum wages starting with 2026 income. Search coverage attributes this to Law 239/2025, published in Monitorul Oficial (MO) no. 1160 of 15 Dec 2025. A cap of 60 minimum wages still applied to 2025 income — [capital.ro CASS 2026](https://www.capital.ro/plata-cass-din-1-ianuarie-2026-noile-calcule-pentru-romanii-cu-venituri-din-activitati-independente.html); [fiscalitatea 24548](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/majorare-cass-din-2026-cine-plateste-mai-mult-si-cine-nu-este-vizat-24548/); [infotva on Law 239/2025](https://infotva.manager.ro/articole/infotva/legea-nr-239-din-2025-principalele-masuri-adoptate-de-pachetul-2-de-masuri-fiscale-24452.html); [capital.ro +4,860 lei CASS](https://www.capital.ro/lovitura-fiscala-pentru-pfa-uri-cine-poate-plati-cu-4-860-de-lei-mai-mult-la-cass.html)
- **Employee exemption from the CASS floor.** If the person has salary income of at least 6 minimum wages (or other income on which CASS was paid at that level), CASS on the PFA is 10% of actual net income, with no top-up to the 6-minimum-wage minimum. Example: 1,000 lei of net income gives 100 lei of CASS instead of 2,430 lei — [fiscalitatea 24125](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/taxe-pfa-2025-ce-obligatii-cas-si-cass-exista-pentru-persoanele-care-au-si-venituri-din-cim-24125/); [contabilul.manager.ro 28414](https://contabilul.manager.ro/a/28414/salariat-cu-norma-intreaga-si-titular-pfa-studiu-de-caz-privind-plata-cass.html); [fiscalitatea 24713](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/taxe-pfa-cas-cass-si-impozit-pe-venit-pentru-pfa-urile-cu-activitati-independente-24713/); [contapp excepții](https://contapp.ro/blog/exceptii-de-la-plata-cas-si-cass/)
  - Conflicts:
    - Fiscalitatea's 2026 texts test the salary in the *previous* year, while a 2025 Fiscalitatea guide and Alfasign test the year covered by the return.
    - One Alfasign example charges CASS only on the excess over the threshold.
    - Capital.ro says no CASS is due below 6 minimum wages.
  - Sources for the conflicts: [alfasign CASS guide](https://alfasign.ro/cass-2025-ghid-complet/); [capital.ro](https://www.capital.ro/plafoane-cas-si-cass-2026-ce-taxe-vor-plati-romanii-dupa-cresterea-salariului-minim.html)
- **Filing.** PFA tax and contributions are reported on the Declarația unică (D212). For 2026 income the deadline is 25 May 2027. A PFA whose 2025 gross receipts exceeded €25,000 must use the real system from 2026 — [contapp taxe PFA 2026](https://contapp.ro/blog/taxe-pfa-2026/)

**Dividend tax**
- The rate is 16% on dividends distributed from 1 Jan 2026, under Law 141/2025 (MO no. 699 of 25 July 2025), which amends art. 43(2) of the Codul fiscal.
  - It is a final tax, declared and paid by the 25th of the month after payment.
  - Distributions approved by 31 Dec 2025 keep the 10% rate.
  - A 2025 profit distributed by a 2026 resolution is taxed at 16%.
  - Sources: [ANAF – Law 141/2025 text](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/L_141_2025.pdf); [ANAF Galați/Vrancea note](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Galati/Vrancea/Modif-imp-dividende-Legea-141-25.pdf); [fiscalitatea 24483](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/impozit-dividende-ce-cota-se-aplica-pentru-dividende-distribuite-in-2025-dar-platite-in-2026-24483/); [fiscalitatea 24319](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/impozit-dividende-cota-de-16-nu-se-va-aplica-in-2025-24319/)
- Rate history: 5% → 8% (2023) → 10% (January 2025) → 16% (2026) — [startco dividende](https://startco.ro/blog/?p=18427)

**CASS on dividends (art. 174-type "other income")**
- **Fixed tiers.** The tiers apply to the combined total of dividends, interest, rent and similar income. At 4,050 lei:
  - below 24,300 lei (6 minimum wages): no CASS;
  - 24,300–48,600 lei: 2,430 lei;
  - 48,600–97,200 lei: 4,860 lei;
  - above 97,200 lei: 9,720 lei, the maximum.
  - The base is the threshold itself, not the actual income. CASS is declared on D212 by 25 May.
  - Sources: [alfasign dividende](https://alfasign.ro/taxe-dividende-srl/); [fiscalitatea 24586](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/cass-pentru-dividende-creste-sau-va-fi-mentinut-la-12-salarii-minime-din-2026-24586/); [permisdeantreprenor](https://permisdeantreprenor.ro/impozit-pe-dividende-2026/)
- The 72-minimum-wage cap applies only to independent-activity income; dividends keep the 6/12/24 tiers — [alfasign dividende](https://alfasign.ro/taxe-dividende-srl/)
- **Conflict: gross or net dividend for the tier test.** Alfasign's example charges 4,860 lei on a 100,000 lei gross dividend, which implies the tiers are tested on the 84,000 lei left after the 16% tax. Bono and Permis de antreprenor also refer to net figures. Other sources are silent — [alfasign dividende](https://alfasign.ro/taxe-dividende-srl/); [bono](https://bono.ro/calculator-dividende)
- **Conflict: whether PFA income is aggregated with dividends for the tiers.** Alfasign says yes; Fiscalitatea's list of categories omits PFA income — [alfasign dividende](https://alfasign.ro/taxe-dividende-srl/); [fiscalitatea 24586](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/cass-pentru-dividende-creste-sau-va-fi-mentinut-la-12-salarii-minime-din-2026-24586/)

**SRL micro-enterprise (microîntreprindere) in 2026**
- **Revenue cap.** The cap is €100,000 from 1 Jan 2026, down from €250,000 in 2025; the reduction was set by OUG 156/2024.
  - It is tested at 31 Dec of the prior year.
  - The turnover of linked enterprises is added in.
  - The lei equivalent is about 509,630–509,850 lei, using a 31 Dec 2025 rate of 5.0963–5.0985 lei/EUR.
  - Sources: [contzilla micro 2026](https://www.contzilla.ro/microintreprinderea-in-2026/); [contzilla plafoane 2026](https://www.contzilla.ro/plafoane-fiscale-2026-micro-tva-intrastat/); [fiscalitatea 24602](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/microintreprindere-in-2026-2-schimbari-importante-pentru-firme-24602/)
- **Rate.** A single 1% rate on revenue applies regardless of CAEN code. The 3% tier was abolished by OUG 89/2025. In 2025 the bands had been 1% up to €60k and 3% from €60k to €250k, with 3% also tied to certain CAEN codes — [startupcafe 92230](https://startupcafe.ro/nou-impozit-micro-2026-scadere-suma-neimpozabila-salariul-minim-monitorul-oficial-descarca-oug-89-2025-trenulet-92230); [KPMG – OUG 89/2025](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ro/pdf/2026/Emergency-Ordinance-89_2025-RO.pdf); [contabilul 29759](https://contabilul.manager.ro/a/29759/impozitarea-microintreprinderilor-2026-ce-modificari-esentiale-a-adus-oug-nr-892025-ordonanta-trenulets.html); [fiscalitatea 24615](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/ghid-practic-impozitarea-microintreprinderilor-incepand-cu-1-ianuarie-2026-24615/)
- **Employee condition.** Any one of the following qualifies:
  - at least one full-time employee;
  - part-time contracts whose hours add up to one full-time position;
  - an administrator paid under a mandate contract, provided the fee is at least the minimum gross wage and the related contributions are paid. One guide says an associate-administrator with a full-time employment contract also counts.
  - Sources: [bono micro](https://bono.ro/blog/impozit-micro-obligatii-fiscale-pentru-microintreprinderi); [startco](https://startco.ro/blog/conditiile-de-aplicare-a-impozitului-micro); [contzilla micro 2026](https://www.contzilla.ro/microintreprinderea-in-2026/)
  - Conflict: some guides say only "at least one employee" without specifying full time.
- **New companies** now have 90 days from registration to hire, up from 30, under OUG 8/2026 of 25 Feb 2026. If they miss the window, they move to profit tax from the following quarter — [fiscalitatea 24722](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/oug-nr-82026-modificari-fiscale-microintreprinderi-tva-la-incasare-bonificatie-3-24722/); [expertmind OUG 8/2026](https://www.expertmind.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Relansare-economica-Noutati-fiscale-conform-OUG-8_2026.pdf); [contabilul 29820](https://contabilul.manager.ro/a/29820/impozit-micro-2026-ghid-practic-conditii-cota-unica-si-ce-obligatii-trebuie-respectate.html)
- **Sick leave.** A sole employee on sick leave still counts, but only while cumulative sick leave in the year stays at 30 days or less — [contabilul 29944](https://contabilul.manager.ro/a/29944/microintreprindere-ce-se-intampla-daca-unicul-salariat-este-in-concediu-medical.html) [single source]
- **Other conditions:**
  - An associate holding more than 25% in several eligible companies can apply the micro regime in only one of them.
  - Annual financial statements must be filed on time.
  - The state and local authorities cannot be shareholders.
  - The company must not be in dissolution.
  - Banking, insurance, gambling and capital-markets activities are excluded.
  - Sources: [contzilla micro 2026](https://www.contzilla.ro/microintreprinderea-in-2026/); [startco](https://startco.ro/blog/conditiile-de-aplicare-a-impozitului-micro)
- **Consultancy cap.** The rule limiting consultancy/management revenue to 20% was repealed from 1 Jan 2025 — [ANAF Brașov micro 2025 guide](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Brasov/Brasov/micro_2025.pdf); [bono micro](https://bono.ro/blog/impozit-micro-obligatii-fiscale-pentru-microintreprinderi)
- **Exceeding the cap mid-year** moves the company to 16% profit tax from the quarter in which the cap is exceeded, with a D700 filing within 15 days — [folositor](https://folositor.ro/ghid/ce-este-microintreprinderea-2026) [single source; this is also the only 2026 source captured for the 16% profit-tax rate]

**SRL registration (ONRC)**
- **Share capital.** New SRLs need a minimum share capital of 500 lei. Existing SRLs whose prior-year net turnover exceeds 400,000 lei must raise capital to 5,000 lei. Both rules come from Law 239/2025 (MO no. 1160, 15 Dec 2025).
  - ONRC's deadline for existing firms is 18 Dec 2027 (a 2027 item).
  - The MO publication fee is cut by 50% for capital increases made until 31 Dec 2026.
  - Start date conflict: 18 Dec 2025 (Alfasign) vs 1 Jan 2026 (Bono, Forvis Mazars).
  - Sources: [alfasign capital 2026](https://alfasign.ro/majorare-capital-social-2026/); [contzilla capital minim](https://www.contzilla.ro/capitalul-social-minim-pachetul-2-de-masuri-fiscale/); [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/ro/en/content/download/1273178/file/3036407)

  - Sources: [bono costuri înființare](https://bono.ro/blog/costuri-infiintare); [bono cum înființezi SRL](https://bono.ro/blog/cum-infiintezi-un-srl)

### Inferences

**Worked Romanian-side net take-home**

Assumptions:
- 2026 rules; minimum wage reference 4,050 lei for contributions; minimum wage for actual pay 4,050 lei (January–June) and 4,325 lei (July–December).
- P = annual pre-owner-pay profit. For micro, revenue R = P, i.e. negligible other expenses; the micro tax is 1% of R, so higher revenue means a slightly higher tax.
- He keeps his full-time salaried job (salary ≥ 6 minimum wages), so the PFA CASS has no floor.

**A) PFA (sistem real)**

CAS = 0 if P < 48,600; 12,150 if 48,600 ≤ P < 97,200; 24,300 if P ≥ 97,200.
CASS = 0.10·P.
Tax = 0.10·(P − CAS − CASS).

Closed forms:
- P < 48,600: net = 0.81·P
- 48,600 ≤ P < 97,200: net = 0.81·P − 10,935
- P ≥ 97,200: net = 0.81·P − 21,870

| Profit P | CAS | CASS | Income tax | Total RO | Net | Burden |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30,000 | 0 | 3,000 | 2,700 | 5,700 | **24,300** | 19.0% |
| 60,000 | 12,150 | 6,000 | 4,185 | 22,335 | **37,665** | 37.2% |
| 120,000 | 24,300 | 12,000 | 8,370 | 44,670 | **75,330** | 37.2% |

- **Cliff trap 1.** At P = 48,599, net is 39,365. At P = 48,600, CAS of 12,150 kicks in and net falls to 28,431, a drop of about 10,934 lei. Net only recovers above about 62,100 lei of profit.
- **Cliff trap 2.** At P = 97,200 there is a second drop of about 10,935 lei. It is recovered at about 110,700 lei.
- Planning implication: either keep PFA profit just below 48,600 or 97,200 lei (for example via legitimate expenses), or accept the jump. The CAS does buy pension contribution periods (stagiu).

**B) SRL micro, with him as administrator on a mandate contract at the minimum wage**

Mandate pay M (2026, full year) = 6×4,050 + 6×4,325 = 50,250 lei.

His personal taxes on M:
- CAS 25% = 12,562.50
- CASS 10% = 5,025
- income tax 10% × (50,250 − 12,562.50 − 5,025) = 3,266.25
- net = 29,396.25

These personal rates and the employer CAM rate of 2.25% (≈ 1,130.63 lei) are **assumptions not verified this session**.

Company:
- distributable D = P − M − CAM − 0.01·P
- dividend tax 16%
- CASS tier tested on the net dividend (base case)

Closed form (for P > ~51,900): net = 0.8316·P − 13,763.48 − CASS_tier.

| P | D | Dividend tax | CASS (dividends) | Net dividend | + net mandate pay | Total net | Burden |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30,000 | −21,681 (loss) | – | – | owner must fund 21,681 | 29,396 | **≈7,716 (not viable)** | ≈74% |
| 60,000 | 8,019 | 1,283 | 0 | 6,736 | 29,396 | **36,133** | 39.8% |
| 120,000 | 67,419 | 10,787 | 4,860 | 51,772 | 29,396 | **81,169** | 32.4% |

- Without CAM, the totals are 37,082 (60k) and 82,118 (120k).
- If the company runs only part of the year, M is prorated. New companies also get 90 days to meet the employee condition.

**C) SRL on 16% profit tax (no employee needed; all profit paid out as dividends)**

Net = P × 0.84 × 0.84 − CASS_tier = 0.7056·P − CASS_tier.

| P | Profit tax 16% | Gross dividend | Dividend tax 16% | Net dividend | CASS (net test / gross test) | Net (net test / gross test) | Burden |

## Q2. VAT: 2026 small-business threshold, B2B services to EU and US clients, art. 317 code, buying foreign SaaS

### Takeaway
- **Threshold.** The small-business VAT exemption threshold is 395,000 lei of annual turnover (since 1 Sept 2025, OG 22/2025), unchanged in 2026.
- **What counts.** Only supplies whose place of supply is Romania count toward it. B2B services to EU and US businesses are taxed where the customer is established, so they neither bear Romanian VAT nor count toward the threshold.
- **Art. 317 code.** A non-VAT-registered PFA or SRL must obtain a special art. 317 VAT code (form D700) *before* the first B2B service sold to, or bought from, the EU. It must then file D390 (recapitulative) and D301 (special VAT return).
- **Foreign SaaS/API purchases.** Buying from the US or EU triggers reverse-charge Romanian VAT (21%, single snippet), payable via D301 and not deductible for a non-VAT-registered business. In effect, foreign tool costs rise by about 21% unless he registers for VAT normally.

### Cited Findings
- **Threshold.** The special exemption regime (regimul special de scutire) has a cap of 395,000 lei, raised from 300,000 lei by OG 22/2025 effective 1 Sept 2025, and unchanged in 2026 — [fiscalitatea plafoane 2026](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/plafoane-fiscale-2026-24595/); [ANAF guide on OG 22/2025 (hosted by startupcafe)](https://startupcafe.ro/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Ghid-ANAF-modificari-plafon-OG-22-2025.pdf); [expertmind](https://www.expertmind.ro/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Modificari-TVA-regimul-de-scutire-pentru-intreprinderi-mici-by-Centrul-de-Afaceri-Expert-Mind.pdf); [zarinacrm](https://www.zarinacrm.ro/plafon-tva-2026-platitor-tva/)
- **What counts toward the threshold:**
  - Only invoices for services whose place of supply is Romania count (Codul fiscal art. 278(2) and art. 310(2)); sales to buyers abroad are excluded.
  - The test runs on invoices issued, not cash received; advance invoices count.
  - Companies founded mid-year face the same 395,000 lei cap.
  - Sources: [infotva 24640](https://infotva.manager.ro/articole/infotva/calcul-plafon-scutire-tva-ce-venituri-intra-in-cifra-de-afaceri-de-referinta-24640.html); [zarinacrm](https://www.zarinacrm.ro/plafon-tva-2026-platitor-tva/)
- **Exceeding the cap.** Registration and the normal regime apply from the date of exceeding. Sources conflict on the filing deadline: "the same day" under OG 22/2025 (zarinacrm) vs the older rule of 10 days after month-end. At exactly 395,000 lei, ANAF's guide treats the business as still qualifying — [zarinacrm](https://www.zarinacrm.ro/plafon-tva-2026-platitor-tva/); [ANAF guide](https://startupcafe.ro/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Ghid-ANAF-modificari-plafon-OG-22-2025.pdf)
- **Art. 317 special code:**
  - Non-normally-registered persons need a valid code for intra-EU services, requested via D700 section B.VI through the online taxpayer account (SPV), *before* the first EU service purchase or supply.
  - If the request is late, VAT is still due from the date of the transaction, and penalties may follow.
  - The code does not make the holder a normal VAT payer.
  - D700 replaced form 091 in March 2022.
  - Sources: [contapp TVA servicii UE/non-UE](https://contapp.ro/blog/tva-servicii-ue-non-ue-neplatitori/); [startco cod TVA intracomunitar](https://startco.ro/blog/cod-tva-intracomunitar-articol/); [bono](https://bono.ro/blog/tva-intracomunitar-cum-obtii-cod-cum-il-verifici-pe-vies-si-cum-platesti-tva-ul)
- **Selling B2B services to EU clients as a non-VAT payer:**
  - The invoice carries no Romanian VAT, shows both VAT codes, and states "taxare inversă" (reverse charge).
  - The sale is reported in D390 under code P.
  - No tax is payable in Romania.
  - If the client has no valid VIES number, the guide treats it as a consumer: no VAT, art. 310 exemption cited, no D390.
  - Sources: [contapp vânzări UE/non-UE](https://contapp.ro/blog/tva-vanzari-ue-non-ue-neplatitori/); [contapp TVA servicii UE/non-UE](https://contapp.ro/blog/tva-servicii-ue-non-ue-neplatitori/)
- **Buying services from the EU:** reported in D390 under code S, with the VAT declared and paid via D301 — [contapp TVA servicii UE/non-UE](https://contapp.ro/blog/tva-servicii-ue-non-ue-neplatitori/)
- **Buying services from outside the EU (e.g. US SaaS/API):**
  - The place of supply is Romania, where the customer is, so the buyer must self-assess Romanian VAT (reverse charge, art. 307(2)) through D301 under its CUI (fiscal registration number).
  - According to ContApp, no art. 317 code or D390 is needed for non-EU suppliers.
  - Risk: if the buyer does not declare business status and tax ID to the platform, the platform may charge VAT as for a consumer (OSS), and the VAT ends up paid twice.
  - Sources: [contapp TVA servicii UE/non-UE](https://contapp.ro/blog/tva-servicii-ue-non-ue-neplatitori/); [contapp D301](https://contapp.ro/blog/declaratia-301-decontul-special-de-tva-tot-ce-trebuie-sa-stii/); [fiscalitatea D301](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/d301-ce-persoane-au-obligatia-de-a-depune-decontul-special-de-tva-22812/); [portalcontabilitate – abonament online din SUA](https://www.portalcontabilitate.ro/achizitie-abonament-online-din-sua-cum-se-declara-tva-221816.htm)
- **D301 timing.** D301 and the related payment are due by the 25th of the following month (art. 326). No D301 is filed for a month without such purchases. A business already normally VAT-registered (art. 316) files D300 instead — [contapp D301](https://contapp.ro/blog/declaratia-301-decontul-special-de-tva-tot-ce-trebuie-sa-stii/)
- **VAT rate.** The rate used in ContApp's 2026 D301 example is 21% — [contapp TVA servicii UE/non-UE](https://contapp.ro/blog/tva-servicii-ue-non-ue-neplatitori/) [single snippet. The increase from 19% to 21% is understood to come from Law 141/2025 from 1 Aug 2025, but that was not captured in a snippet; verify]
- **Optional registration.** Normal VAT registration by option exists — [avocatnet – înregistrare prin opțiune](https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_24164/ANAF-Inregistrarea-in-scopuri-de-TVA-prin-optiune.html) [title-level evidence]
- **B2C EU threshold.** A 2026 article covers the obligations above and below the €10,000 EU-wide threshold for intra-EU B2C distance supplies — [infotva 24496](https://infotva.manager.ro/articole/legislatie/tva-intracomunitar-in-2026-ce-obligatii-au-firmele-atunci-cand-plafonul-este-subpeste-10000-euro-24496.html) [title only; content not captured]

## Q3. US side for a US citizen with a Romanian PFA: totalization, SE tax, FEIE, FTC, forms

### Takeaway
- **Totalization.** The **US–Romania Social Security (totalization) Agreement entered into force on 1 September 2026**. This is new: older expat guides and SSA pages say no agreement exists.
- **Coverage for his case.** The agreement covers US SECA taxes, i.e. both the 12.4% OASDI and 2.9% Medicare parts. For self-employment carried on in Romania by someone living in Romania, the SSA pamphlet (residence rule) and the treaty text and SSA's operating manual (place-of-work rule) both assign coverage to Romania only.
- **What he needs.** With a certificate of coverage from Romania's National House of Public Pensions (CNPP), PFA income for periods covered by the agreement should be exempt from US SE tax.
- **Open question.** How the US treats self-employment income earned in calendar 2026 before 1 September is **unconfirmed**.
- **FEIE.** The 2026 exclusion is $132,900. It removes US income tax on his combined salary and PFA earnings at his scale, but it never reduces SE tax.
- **Without agreement relief,** US SE tax would cost about 14.1% of PFA profit on top of the Romanian taxes, taking the total burden to about 33–51%.

- **Disregarded SRL.** If he elected disregarded status, the business income would be self-employment income for US purposes. Under the same territoriality rule it should be Romanian-covered, so no US SE tax with a certificate. This mirrors the PFA but keeps the SRL's Romanian tax layers.
- **Illustrative US cost at 120k lei of profit, using the UNVERIFIED parameters listed under Gaps:**
  - **Micro.** Tested income is about 67.4k lei after the mandate pay. If the 1% turnover tax is not creditable, a §962 election leaves about 12.6% residual US tax, roughly 8.5k lei. Romanian-plus-US net would be about 72.7k lei, below PFA's 75.3k, and that is before fees.
  - **Profit-tax SRL.** 16% × 90% = 14.4%, which is more than the 12.6% NCTI rate, so there should be about zero residual US NCTI tax with §962. Dividends would face the 16% Romanian dividend tax, which can be credited against US qualified-dividend tax (if the SRL counts as a qualified foreign corporation). Net is about 79.8k lei, minus 5471/962 compliance fees that likely exceed the 4.5k lei advantage.

### Gaps
**UNVERIFIED framework from prior knowledge; not searched; confirm with a US expat preparer or a follow-up research pass.**

1. **Classification:**
   - A Romanian SRL is believed *not* to be on the per se corporation list in Treas. Reg. §301.7701-2(b)(8); Romania's listed per se form is believed to be the "Societate pe Actiuni" (SA). The SRL is therefore an "eligible entity."
   - Default for a foreign eligible entity whose members all have limited liability: association, i.e. taxed as a corporation (Reg. §301.7701-3(b)(2)(i)(B)).
   - Election to be disregarded: Form 8832 (retroactive by at most 75 days; late-election relief under Rev. Proc. 2009-41). After that, an annual Form 8858.
   - The owner of a disregarded entity is treated as self-employed for SE tax (Reg. §301.7701-2(c)(2)(iv)).
2. **CFC and Form 5471:**
   - As a corporation owned 100% by a US person, the SRL is a CFC.
   - Form 5471 is due annually: Categories 4 and 5, plus Category 2/3 in the year of formation or acquisition.
   - Penalty: $10,000 per form per year (IRC §6038(b)), plus $10,000 per 30 days after an IRS notice, up to $50,000 more. A foreign tax credit reduction (§6038(c)) is also possible.
   - The statute of limitations for the whole return stays open until the form is filed (§6501(c)(8)).
3. **Subpart F, foreign base company services income (FBCSI, §954(e)):**
   - FBCSI requires both (i) services performed for or on behalf of a *related* person and (ii) services performed *outside* the CFC's country of organization.
   - Services performed in Romania for unrelated Romanian, EU or US clients should therefore not be FBCSI.
   - Watch the "substantial assistance" rule (Reg. §1.954-4(b)(1)(iv), Notice 2007-13).
   - Software *licensing* income could be foreign personal holding company royalties unless the active-business exception for unrelated persons applies (§954(c)(2)(A)). SaaS vs license classification (Reg. §1.861-18; proposed §1.861-19) also matters.
4. **NCTI (formerly GILTI):**
   - P.L. 119-21 ("One Big Beautiful Bill Act," enacted 4 July 2025) renamed GILTI "net CFC tested income" (NCTI) for tax years beginning after 31 Dec 2025.
   - The QBAI (10% tangible return) offset is eliminated.
   - The §250 deduction becomes 40%, an effective 12.6% corporate rate.
   - The deemed-paid credit rises to 90% (from 80%).
   - **An individual without §962:** NCTI is taxed at ordinary rates, with no deemed-paid credit and no FEIE (it is not earned income).
   - **With a §962 election:** NCTI is taxed at 21% less the §250 deduction (availability for §962 electors under Reg. §1.962-1(b)(1)(i)(B)(3) needs reconfirming after the 2025 law), less 90% of foreign income taxes. The break-even foreign rate is about 14%.
5. **Creditability:**
   - Romania's micro tax is 1% of *revenue*. It is likely **not** a creditable income tax, because a gross-receipts tax fails the net-income/net-gain requirement of Reg. §1.901-2 even under the Notice 2023-55/2023-80 relief. So a micro SRL's NCTI is effectively untaxed abroad and bears US tax.
   - The 16% Romanian profit tax is creditable.
6. **Distributions:**
   - Earnings already taxed in the US are excluded from US tax on distribution (§959).
   - For §962 inclusions, §962(d) taxes distributions to the extent they exceed the US tax already paid.
   - Qualified-dividend rates (0/15/20%) apply if the SRL is a "qualified foreign corporation," i.e. eligible for a comprehensive treaty that has an exchange-of-information program. The US–Romania 1973 treaty is believed to be on the IRS-approved list (Notice 2011-64); confirm.
   - The Romanian 16% dividend tax is creditable on Form 1116; the basket is to be confirmed.
7. **FICA:**
   - Wages from a Romanian SRL taxed as a corporation are not subject to US FICA. It is not an "American employer" (IRC §3121(h)) and has no §3121(l) agreement.
   - Wages are not self-employment income.
   - Wages for work in Romania are foreign earned income eligible for the FEIE.
8. **Compliance fees:**
   - Form 5471 is commonly priced by expat firms at roughly $1,000–2,500+ per form per year; Form 8858 at roughly $500–1,000. These are **unverified estimates; get quotes** (Greenback, Bright!Tax, Taxes for Expats, Creative Planning).
   - Romanian SRL accounting (double-entry bookkeeping, financial statements, D100/D205, payroll) costs more than PFA bookkeeping.
9. **Other SRL owner reporting:**
   - FBAR for the SRL's accounts (financial interest via more than 50% ownership, plus signature authority).
   - Form 8938, where the SRL interest is reported via Form 5471.

### Gaps
**UNVERIFIED framework from prior knowledge; confirm every article number and wording.**

1. **Side business is permitted in principle:**
   - Codul muncii (Law 53/2003, republished) art. 35(1) lets an employee hold jobs with several employers.
   - Law 283/2022 (transposing Directive (EU) 2019/1152, art. 9 on parallel employment) is believed to bar employers from prohibiting work for others outside working hours, subject to objective-ground limits such as conflicts of interest or confidentiality. Exact article and wording to be confirmed.
   - Running a PFA or SRL is not an employment contract, so limits come from his contract clauses and his statutory duties.
2. **Contract clauses and duties:**
   - Art. 20 lists specific clauses: training, non-compete, mobility and confidentiality.
   - An "exclusivity" clause is not a named clause. Its enforceability after Law 283/2022 is doubtful unless objectively justified.
   - Art. 26 covers confidentiality during and after employment, with damages for breach.
   - Art. 39(2)(d) is the duty of fidelity (obligația de fidelitate) towards the employer in performing duties; art. 39(2)(f) is the duty of professional secrecy (secretul de serviciu).
   - Competing with the employer during employment can breach the fidelity duty and lead to disciplinary sanctions up to dismissal, plus material liability (art. 253ff).
3. **Post-employment non-compete (art. 21–24):**
   - Valid only if the contract specifies the prohibited activities, the third parties concerned, the geographic area, the period and the monthly indemnity.
   - The indemnity must be at least 50% of the average gross salary income of the last 6 months.
   - Maximum duration is 2 years after termination.
   - It cannot amount to an absolute ban on exercising the profession; a court can reduce it.
   - It does not take effect in certain termination cases (e.g. some terminations by law, or dismissal for reasons not related to the employee).
   - It applies *after* the employment ends; while employed, the fidelity duty governs.
4. **Software ownership, Legea 8/1996 (copyright):**
   - Special rule: "În lipsa unei clauze contrare, drepturile patrimoniale de autor asupra programelor pentru calculator create de unul sau mai mulți angajați în exercitarea atribuțiilor de serviciu sau după instrucțiunile celui care angajează aparțin acestuia din urmă" ("Unless otherwise agreed, the economic rights in computer programs created by one or more employees in the course of their duties, or on the employer's instructions, belong to the employer").
   - This was **art. 74 in the pre-2018 numbering**. The law was republished in June 2018 and the article was likely renumbered, so the current number must be confirmed.
   - Unlike other works (where the general rule leaves rights with the employee-author unless agreed otherwise), software defaults to the employer.
   - Ideas, procedures, methods of operation, mathematical concepts and the principles underlying any element of a program (including interfaces) are not protected (old art. 73(2)). He can reuse general know-how and architecture *ideas*, but not the code, documentation, training data or confidential business information.
   - Copyright assignment contracts must be in writing as proof; the article is to be confirmed.
5. **Trade secrets:** Law 11/1991 on unfair competition, as amended to transpose Directive (EU) 2016/943, protects confidential business information. The employer's curated procurement-document corpus, prompts, evaluation sets, client lists and pricing likely qualify.
6. **Clean commercialization paths:**
   - **(a) Written licence or assignment** from the employer for the chatbot code and related IP. Specify scope, territory, exclusivity, royalty or fee, and whether he may target the employer's clients.
   - **(b) Written side-project or spin-off agreement or consent.** Define permitted activities, IP ownership of new work, non-solicitation of clients and staff, and confidentiality.
   - **(c) Clean-room rebuild:**
     - new code written from scratch on his own time and equipment, with his own accounts and API keys;
     - no employer code, data, prompts, embeddings or documents;
     - provenance documented through dated git history;
     - publicly available public-procurement data (SEAP/e-licitatie) used only directly from public sources;
     - no targeting of the employer's clients during employment.
   - **(d) Read his employment contract and internal policies first** (IP, confidentiality, conflict of interest, non-compete clauses).
7. **Risks of not doing so:**
   - an injunction and damages for copyright infringement;
   - a trade-secret or unfair-competition claim;
   - disciplinary dismissal for breach of the fidelity duty;
   - possible criminal exposure for unauthorized copying of data or systems (not researched).
