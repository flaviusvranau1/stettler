## 1. SME digitalization programs in 2026: status, budget, grant/company, co-financing, eligible expenses, deadlines, number of beneficiaries

### Takeaway
On 8 Oct 2026 no SME-digitalization grant call is open that a Nord-Vest company can apply to.
- **PNRR "Digitalizarea IMM-urilor"** (20k–100k EUR) is in close-out. Implementation ended 31 Jul 2026 and final aid payments are due by 30 Oct 2026.
- **ADR Nord-Vest digitalization call PRNV/2023/121/1** (10k–100k EUR, about 16.8M EUR) closed to submissions on 25 Nov 2024. Its projects were still in evaluation or contracting in mid-2025, and no 2026 status was found. Its winners are therefore the likely live regional buyer pool.
- **2026 digitalization calls in other regions** (Centru, Vest, Nord-Est, București-Ilfov) are not open to Nord-Vest firms.
- **Ministry of Economy (MEDAT) programs in 2026** are a restart of an old round (Femeia Antreprenor 2024), Start-Up Nation IV (new micro-firms), or SME Eco-Tech (Swiss funds, manufacturing-first, undersubscribed). No national 2026 "digitalization voucher" was found.

#### PNRR "Digitalizarea IMM-urilor" (PNRR Component 9; MIPE; partner banks CEC Bank and BRD)
- [OFF] MIPE's page for Order 607 of 28.04.2026, which amends the applicant guide, describes the scheme as a "grant de până la 100.000 euro pe întreprindere care să sprijine IMM-urile în adoptarea tehnologiilor digitale". Source: [mfe.gov.ro](https://mfe.gov.ro/pnrr-ordinul-nr-607-28-04-2026-pentru-modificarea-ghidului-solicitantului-digitalizarea-imm-urilor-grant-de-pana-la-100-000-euro-pe-intreprindere-care-sa-sprijine-imm-urile-in-adoptarea-tehnologii/).
- [PRESS] Grant band is 20,000–100,000 EUR per firm, and the guide was still being amended by order in 2026. Source: [StartupCafe 94157](https://startupcafe.ro/granturi-pnrr-pentru-digitalizare-imm-ordin-2026-de-modificare-a-ghidului-la-finantarile-de-cate-20-000-100-000-eur-94157).
- [PRESS] When the call opened in Feb 2023 the budget was 347.5M EUR. It drew 7,188 applications against an original target of 5,384 contracts. Sources: [StartupCafe 80106](https://startupcafe.ro/rezultate-fonduri-pnrr-digitalizare-imm-ministerul-a-contractat-2-828-firme-jumatate-din-obiectiv-la-2-ani-si-2-luni-de-la-lansarea-programului-7-liste-de-proiecte-admise-si-situatia-la-zi-80106); "7,188 projects, 30% above target" per [Financial Intelligence](https://financialintelligence.ro/pnrr-digitalizarea-imm-urilor-peste-7-188-proiecte-depuse-cu-30-mai-mult-decat-tinta-stabilita/).
- [PRESS] (2025) Two years and two months after launch, only 2,828 contracts were signed, 52.5% of target. Source: [StartupCafe 80106](https://startupcafe.ro/rezultate-fonduri-pnrr-digitalizare-imm-ministerul-a-contractat-2-828-firme-jumatate-din-obiectiv-la-2-ani-si-2-luni-de-la-lansarea-programului-7-liste-de-proiecte-admise-si-situatia-la-zi-80106).
- [PRESS] The latest count found is 4,559 contracts worth about 175M EUR (876.3M lei). The headline also says "hundreds of millions EUR" of the scheme were reallocated to other PNRR objectives. Source: [StartupCafe 88885](https://startupcafe.ro/rezultate-pnrr-sute-milioane-euro-digitalizare-imm-realocati-alte-obiective-liste-firme-ong-contractate-finantare-88885) (1 src). Conflicting figure: another StartupCafe piece attributes 4,460 contracts to MIPE official George Turtoi. Source: [StartupCafe 83632](https://startupcafe.ro/program-digitalizare-imm-ministru-pislaru-discutat-sefii-cec-si-brd-suplimenteze-personal-grabi-aprobari-absurd-83632).
- [CONS] Co-financing: a BRD presentation gives grant = 90% of eligible expenses and 10% own co-financing. Source: [BRD PDF](https://www.brd.ro/sites/default/files/_files/pdf/DIGITALIZARE%20IMM_PNRR.pdf). A CRM vendor's table seems to list 10–30%, so the two conflict. Source: [zarinacrm](https://www.zarinacrm.ro/fonduri-digitalizare-imm/).
- [PRESS] Eligible expenses in the consolidated guide (as republished by StartupCafe, 2022–23 version): company presentation website, new domain name, cloud services, cybersecurity services, ICT hardware, and software including e-commerce solutions. Source: [StartupCafe 22247](https://startupcafe.ro/granturi-100000-euro-ghid-consolidat-program-digitalizare-imm-finantat-pnnr-htm-22247). One snippet adds that orders placed by e-mail do not count as e-commerce for the DESI-type indicators; same source (1 src).
- [PRESS] Consultancy that identifies the technical solutions is eligible only if those solutions are then bought within the project. After the April 2026 amendment, consultancy done before the application also counts. Sources: [StartupCafe 98671](https://startupcafe.ro/granturi-pnrr-digitalizare-prelungire-termene-implementare-proiecte-imm-ong-ordin-mipe-607-609-2026-98671); [Order 607/2026 PDF](https://startupcafe.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Ordinul-607-2026-IMM.pdf).
- [PRESS] OUG 45/2025 (published 12 Sep 2025) made IT-consultancy costs reimbursable under consultancy contracts already in force. The snippet cut off before the conditions. Source: [StartupCafe ?p=86692](https://startupcafe.ro/?p=86692) (1 src).
- [PRESS] Deadlines (2026), sources [StartupCafe 98671](https://startupcafe.ro/granturi-pnrr-digitalizare-prelungire-termene-implementare-proiecte-imm-ong-ordin-mipe-607-609-2026-98671), [StartupCafe 102090](https://startupcafe.ro/granturi-digitalizare-imm-minister-ordin-964-2026-prelungeste-perioada-implementare-proiecte-reguli-cereri-transfer-102090), [StartupCafe 102414](https://startupcafe.ro/granturi-pnrr-digitalizare-imm-prelungit-termen-plata-ajutoare-100000-euro-atentie-cerere-prelugire-implementare-proiect-102414), [Order 964 PDF](https://startupcafe.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ordin-MIPE-prelungire-implementare-digi-IMM.pdf):
  - Order 607/2026 moved the implementation cap from 29 May to 30 Jun 2026.
  - Order 964/2026 (23 Jun 2026) allows implementation up to 31 Jul 2026, on the beneficiary's request.
  - Order 606/2026 moved the aid-payment deadline from 31 Dec 2025 to 31 Aug 2026.
  - Order 963/2026 moved it again to 30 Oct 2026, within the de minimis scheme budget.
- [PRESS] Close-out rules, sources [StartupCafe 102090](https://startupcafe.ro/granturi-digitalizare-imm-minister-ordin-964-2026-prelungeste-perioada-implementare-proiecte-reguli-cereri-transfer-102090) and [StartupCafe 102414](https://startupcafe.ro/granturi-pnrr-digitalizare-imm-prelungit-termen-plata-ajutoare-100000-euro-atentie-cerere-prelugire-implementare-proiect-102414):
  - Only expenses paid in full during implementation count toward the final claim.
  - If a transfer request could not be authorised or paid in time for reasons not caused by the firm, the firm could still pay suppliers after implementation, but not after 31 Jul 2026.
  - Transfer requests for upcoming payments had to be filed at least 90 calendar days before the implementation deadline.
  - Extension requests filed after the current period lapsed are not considered.
- [PRESS] A guide version reported by StartupCafe says that if the year's budget credits are insufficient, the beneficiary is notified and payments stop until funds are secured. Source: [StartupCafe 102414](https://startupcafe.ro/granturi-pnrr-digitalizare-imm-prelungit-termen-plata-ajutoare-100000-euro-atentie-cerere-prelugire-implementare-proiect-102414) (1 src).
- [PRESS] About 4,500 beneficiaries were still implementing projects as the June 2026 cutoff approached. Source: [StartupCafe 98671](https://startupcafe.ro/granturi-pnrr-digitalizare-prelungire-termene-implementare-proiecte-imm-ong-ordin-mipe-607-609-2026-98671) (1 src).
- [CONS] After completion there is a 3–5 year durability period (maintenance, reporting, monitoring visits; exact term set in the contract). The vendor tracker was updated 18 Aug 2026. Source: [zarinacrm PNRR page](https://www.zarinacrm.ro/fonduri-digitalizare-imm/pnrr-digitalizare/) (1 src, vendor).
- [PRESS] MIPE published an official Q&A list on implementation for this scheme. Source: [StartupCafe 83507](https://startupcafe.ro/granturi-pnrr-digitalizare-imm-lista-oficiala-intrebari-raspunsuri-implementare-proiecte-intalnire-online-beneficiari-mipe-83507).

## 2. How IT suppliers win contracts from private grant beneficiaries: procurement rules, vendor budgets, consultants and fees, payment timing and risks

### Takeaway
- **Procurement rules.** Private beneficiaries of cohesion-policy grants (Regional Programmes such as PR Nord-Vest, and POCIDIF) buy under MIPE Order 1284/2016, the competitive procedure for private beneficiaries. Notices are published on the "Achiziții Beneficiari Privați" platform (beneficiar.fonduri-ue.ro). The order was still applied in 2026 and was amended by Order 1143/2026 after a July 2026 cyberattack took the platform down for about a month. PNRR private beneficiaries followed a separate route (Order 372/2024), with checks done by the partner banks.
- **How suppliers get in.** In practice they enter at the project-design stage through consultants, or through vendor content marketing that targets grant applicants. PNRR even makes "identifying the technical solution" consultancy an eligible cost when that solution is then bought.
- **Payment risk.** Cash comes late and is conditional. The PNRR digitalization scheme had chronic transfer-request backlogs, a 2025 petition co-signed by suppliers, and press headlines about suppliers pressing for unpaid invoices. Expenses not fully paid inside the implementation window, or bought against procurement rules, become ineligible.

### Cited Findings

#### Procurement rules for private beneficiaries
- [OFF/legal DB] Order MIPE 1284/2016 approves the competitive procedure that private applicants and beneficiaries use to award supply, service and works contracts financed from EU funds. It has been in force since 12 Aug 2016 (Monitorul Oficial 618). Source: [Rubinian](https://www.rubinian.com/ordinul-1284-2016-procedura-competitiva-beneficiari-privati-fonduri-europene).
- [OFF] The order is still applied to 2021–2027 projects in 2026:
  - ADR Nord-Vest published a Mar 2026 document on procurement inside projects and the errors most often found in competitive procedures. Source: [nord-vest.ro PDF](https://www.nord-vest.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Achizitiile-initiate-in-cadrul-proiectelor.pdf).
  - ADR Centru held a Jun 2026 Just Transition beneficiary seminar on procurement under the order. Source: [ADR Centru PTJ PDF](https://ptj.adrcentru.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/1.Seminar-tehnic-beneficiari_Achizitii_PDDTJ.pdf).
  - ADR Muntenia's procurement guide (16 Apr 2026) lists the order with its amendments. Source: [ADR Muntenia PDF](https://www.adrmuntenia.ro/download_file/article/1466/Indreptar-achizitii-publice-16aprilie2026.pdf).
- [legal DB, snippet] The governing principles are transparency, economy, efficiency and effectiveness. Notices and technical specifications are published on beneficiar.fonduri-ue.ro, and breaches of publicity duties are penalised. Source: [Rubinian](https://www.rubinian.com/ordinul-1284-2016-procedura-competitiva-beneficiari-privati-fonduri-europene).
- [OFF, 2019, 2014–2020 era] When estimating contract value, purchases are aggregated if they have the same object, a similar purpose, or target operators in the same relevant market. This prevents splitting a purchase to stay under a threshold. Source: [ADR Sud-Vest Oltenia private-procurement guide](https://por2014-2020.adroltenia.ro/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/GHID-ACHIZITII-4-2019-PRIVATI-ADR-SV-cu-coperta.pdf) (1 src).

  - Under the old regime (Order 1120/2013 instruction, 2014), direct purchase was allowed below 30,000 EUR for goods and services and 100,000 EUR for works. This is obsolete. Source: [2014 instruction PDF](https://media.hotnews.ro/media_server1/document-2014-06-17-17498412-0-instructiune-ordinul-1120-11.pdf).

Whether they bind private beneficiaries was not confirmed. ### Inferences
- Realistic routes for a part-time supplier in Bistrița:
  - (a) Partner with Nord-Vest grant consultants who need a technical solution and quotes when they write projects.
  - (b) Monitor beneficiar.fonduri-ue.ro for NV tender notices from call-121 winners (website, e-commerce, CRM/ERP, custom software, automation, AI).
  - (c) Sell directly to new Start-Up Nation IV and Diaspora Investește Acasă firms, which have deadlines to spend.
- Above the (unconfirmed) threshold, contracts are won through a published competitive procedure, not by relationship alone. Writing a client's technical specification and then bidding against it may draw conflict-of-interest scrutiny in ex-post checks. The specific conflict-of-interest clauses were not retrieved.
- Cash-flow protection (practical inference): invoice against the beneficiary's own co-financing or an advance at signing, use milestone billing, and avoid contracts where payment depends on the state reimbursing the beneficiary. The PNRR backlog shows delays of many months.
- Grant-funded tickets are lumpy (tens of thousands of EUR at most per firm, much of it hardware). For a solo developer at 10–15 h/week, subcontracting the software or website part of larger packages to consultants or integrators is probably more realistic than bidding on whole packages.

### Gaps

## 4. Realistic timeline from application to cash, success/rejection rates, obligations, clawback risks

### Takeaway
Romanian SME grants typically take **1.5 to 3.5 years** from application to final cash:
- PNRR digitalization: call opened Feb 2023, contracts trickled in over 2025–2026, final payments due by 30 Oct 2026.
- Femeia Antreprenor: the 2024 round was only contracted in mid-2026.
- PR NV call 121: projects filed by Nov 2024 were still in evaluation in mid-2025.

Published success rates are modest:

| Scheme | Rate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| PNRR digitalization | ~63% | applicants contracted |
| Femeia Antreprenor 2024 | ~15% | applicants funded |
| PR Nord-Vest, programme-wide | ~24% | projects contracted by Aug 2026, many still in evaluation |
| SME Eco-Tech | undersubscribed | 369 applicants vs ≥800 target |

Obligations typically run for 3–5 years and cover assets, ownership and jobs (2 jobs for 24–30 months in Start-Up Nation). Clawback or ineligibility is triggered mainly by expenses not fully paid inside the implementation window, by non-compliant procurement, and by missed deadlines.

### Inferences
- **For him as an applicant.** The earliest realistic cash from Start-Up Nation IV, if he is eligible (under 30), is 2027, and probably only after he has spent the money and hired 2 people. From Diaspora Investește Acasă, if eligible, the earliest is 2027, after a bank loan has financed a completed investment. Neither fits a less-than-5,000-lei, 10–15 h/week side business.
- **For him as a supplier.** Payment comes from the beneficiary's own funds or after a transfer or reimbursement request clears. PNRR showed many months of backlog, so he should either price the risk (advance payments, milestones, no deferral to reimbursement) or work through consultants or integrators who carry the client relationship.
- **Timing.** The best timing is the implementation windows. For call 121 this is roughly 2026–2027 (inferred from contracting in 2025–2026, not verified). For Start-Up Nation IV, contracts run up to 12 months with a hard stop of 8 Dec 2027. Beneficiaries then must spend fast, which favours suppliers that can deliver standard packages (site, webshop, CRM, automation) quickly.
