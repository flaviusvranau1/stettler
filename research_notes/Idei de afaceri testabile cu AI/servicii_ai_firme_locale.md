## 1. AI chatbots and AI voice receptionists for Romanian SMEs: providers, prices, Romanian-language voice AI quality and cost, WhatsApp Business Platform pricing for Romania (2026)

### Takeaway
Romanian-language AI receptionists and chatbots are already sold by a crowded field of local and international vendors. Public prices run about €79–299/month for SME or clinic voice packages (up to €999 for high volume) and 99–259 lei/month for self-serve text chatbots. Underlying voice-AI costs are about $0.07–0.33/min all-in. Since 1 Oct 2026, WhatsApp Business Platform replies to Romanian numbers are billed at about €0.0239/message once the first 1,000 service messages per number per month are used. That makes high-volume WhatsApp bots materially more expensive to run, while low-volume local businesses stay near zero.

### Inferences
- Voice margins are thin unless real usage stays well under the cap (my arithmetic). Callio Starter (€189, ~700 min if the mapping is right) would cost $70–210 in usage at $0.10–0.30/min if every minute were used. Vocalyy Starter (€299, 1,000 min) would cost $100–300. Packages therefore need minute caps and per-minute overage.
- WhatsApp cost should be negligible for a typical single clinic or salon. Illustration (my numbers): 300 conversations × 6 bot messages = 1,800 service messages, so about 800 billable × €0.0239 ≈ €19/month. High-volume e-commerce support, by contrast, is now a real cost line (≈€690 for 30k messages).
  - Each separate bot message is billed, so bots should send fewer, longer replies.
  - Since 1 Oct 2026, a client whose BSP has no valid payment method on file stops sending after the free 1,000 messages. That's a practical support and churn risk.
- Meta's general-purpose AI ban doesn't cover SME customer-service bots, but 2025–26 showed the platform rules shift fast (policy changes, the October 2026 pricing change, removed IG tags). That is platform risk an agency can't control.
- Instagram: building one multi-client app means App Review as an "app for other businesses". A solo developer may find it simpler to deploy through an existing BSP or omnichannel tool, or to set up each client's own app.
- Romanian speech recognition on 8 kHz phone audio (names, numbers, "da/nu") is the main technical quality risk for voice. The only Romanian evidence is vendor claims and one anecdote, so any voice offer needs per-client acceptance testing.
- Fit (judged, not researched): a text chatbot on website, WhatsApp or IG with RAG over a client's FAQ/prices sits closest to the developer's existing experience (a support chatbot already built) and costs little to run. Voice adds number provisioning (ID and address documents), latency tuning and 24/7 reliability expectations. Those expectations clash with 10–15 h/week of availability.

## 2. Incumbent tools that reduce the value of a custom bot: booking platforms and omnichannel CRMs (prices, Romanian adoption)

### Takeaway
In the two clearest verticals, booking and reminder software is already embedded:
- dentistry: iStoma, with ~2,700 users in 2024 and "5,300 dentists" across RO and 4 other countries per a later bank release;
- beauty: MERO, with >1,000 salons and >3,000 specialists; Calendis from €8/month at launch.

AI-receptionist vendors present themselves as add-ons that integrate with these systems rather than replacements. Docplanner shows no Romanian brand. Kommo ($15–25/user/month, or $25/$35 for new customers from Sept 2026) and Bitrix24 (~$49–69/month for 5 users) bundle omnichannel inboxes and bots, but their Romanian adoption numbers weren't found.

## 4. Accounting niche: obligations 2024–2026, documented pain points, market size (CECCAR and firms), existing tools and prices, gaps a small developer could fill

### Takeaway
Since 2024, Romanian firms have faced a dense and still-changing digital reporting regime:
- e-Factura B2B mandatory since 2024, now covering B2C too, with "5 working days" from 2026;
- Law 88/2026 removing the obligation for CNP-identified individuals from 1 June 2026;
- SAF-T D406 for small taxpayers since 2025, plus the "Active" section in 2026;
- e-Transport fully enforced, with fines rising;
- the e-TVA pre-filled VAT return.

This creates real workload for ~12–13k accounting firms and ~48k CECCAR members. But the most obvious tooling gap, bulk SPV download and archiving for many client companies, is already crowded (15+ tools, often 2–29 lei per company per month, several free tiers), and mainstream suites (SmartBill, Oblio at €29/year, FGO from 15 lei/month, Keez) bundle SPV sync and even bank matching. What's left is narrower (reconciliation edge cases, OCR of non-e-Factura documents, feeding desktop Saga/WinMentor, pre-filing checks), and the evidence on how much accountants would pay for those is thin.

## 5. ANAF API access for third-party apps (OAuth registration, SPV/e-Factura API, rate limits, certificates) and legal/security obligations (GDPR, contracts)

### Takeaway
Any developer holding a Romanian qualified certificate can integrate the e-Factura and e-Transport web services through ANAF's OAuth2 app registration. Access tokens last ~90 days and refresh tokens ~365 days (third-party docs, live-checked July 2026). Quotas are documented, e.g. 1,500 list calls per CUI per day, 10 downloads per message per day and a 60-day message window. Legally, a tool that touches clients' fiscal data makes the developer a GDPR processor or sub-processor under the accountant, which requires Art. 28 contracts. SPV access itself runs on the accountant's own certificate and delegations, and a new ANAF portal and SPV are announced with no date and could break integrations.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] e-Factura web services cover upload, status (stareMesaj), message list (listaMesajeFactura) and download (descarcare). Authentication is either a certificate on each call or OAuth2. — [ANAF e-Factura services](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/Informatii_R/Servicii_web/url_eFactura.html); [ANAF API presentation (PDF)](https://mfinante.gov.ro/static/10/eFactura/prezentare%20api%20efactura.pdf). e-Transport has its own services. — [ANAF e-Transport services](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/Informatii_R/Servicii_web/url_eTransport.html)
- [3RD-PARTY DOC] API details from library docs: [anafpy e-Factura API](https://anafpy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/anaf-reference/efactura/api/)
  - The list call takes a `cif` parameter, so a tool must loop client by client.
  - listaMesajeFactura accepts a window of 1–60 days; listaMesajePaginatieFactura takes start and end times in Unix milliseconds plus a page number.
  - The download returns a ZIP with two XML files.
  - A status query past its daily limit returns an error element with no status value.
- [OFFICIAL file + 3RD-PARTY table] ANAF publishes an API call-limits file. — [mfinante "Limite Apeluri API"](https://mfinante.gov.ro/static/10/eFactura/limiteApeluriAPI.txt)
  - Per a Laravel SDK's "ANAF Official Rate Limits" table: simple list 1,500/CUI/day; paginated list 100,000/CUI/day; download 10 per message ID per day; status 100 per message ID per day; upload 1,000/CUI/day; global 1,000 calls per minute. — [laravel-efactura-sdk](https://root.packagist.org/packages/bee-coded/laravel-efactura-sdk)
  - The list cap (1,500/day/CUI) and the 10 downloads per message also show up in the search summary of ANAF's own documents. The time of the daily reset is unknown.
- [ACADEMIC] Responses and invoice files can be downloaded only within 60 days. — [UTCluj ATN paper](https://users.utcluj.ro/~atn/papers/ATN_2_2025_3.pdf)
- [3RD-PARTY DOC, partly live-probed July 2026] OAuth2 setup, per [anafpy OAuth](https://anafpy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/anaf-reference/oauth/authentication/):
  - Prerequisites: a qualified e-signature certificate on a USB/PKCS#11 token from a Romanian trust service provider, enrolled in SPV.
  - Registration path: anaf.ro → Servicii Online → Înregistrare utilizatori → Dezvoltatori aplicații → Înregistrare pentru API-uri, confirmed with an emailed code.
  - OAuth profile: app name, callback URLs (https only, even for localhost), and the service (E-Factura, E-Transport or both). "Generare Client ID" then issues a client ID and secret.
  - Flow: Authorization Code grant at logincert.anaf.ro/anaf-oauth2/v1 (authorize, token, revoke); HTTP Basic auth at the token endpoint; tokens are JWTs.
  - Lifetimes: the auth code lasts ~1 minute, access tokens 90 days, refresh tokens 365 days. Each refresh returns a new token pair.
  - The certificate is needed only for the one-time browser login. Revocation is via "Renunțare Oauth", and the revoke endpoint needed the certificate login.
- [PRESS/trade, date not visible] ANAF lets subscribers who manage several CUIs use SPV services through independent apps or their accounting/ERP software, provided they log in with a qualified certificate. — [CECCAR Business Magazine](https://www.ceccarbusinessmagazine.ro/anaf-noi-facilitati-pentru-abonatii-spv-care-gestioneaza-simultan-mai-multe-coduri-unice-de-identificare/a/Q9yA48cIW7Aq2Ur30PSi); [Profit.ro](https://profit.ro/taxe-si-consultanta/anaf-introduce-facilitati-pentru-cei-care-gestioneaza-mai-multe-cui-uri-prin-spv-informatiile-vor-putea-fi-accesate-si-prin-aplicatiile-de-de-contabilitate-erp-17907542)
- [VENDOR guides] Certificates and delegation:
  - The certificate belongs to a natural person. One certificate can serve several firms if its holder is the legal representative or an authorised person (împuternicit) for each.
  - A general power of attorney (signed by the legal representative or notarised) gives an accountant SPV access.
  - SPV's "Delegări acces" setting assigns view, filing or payment rights to the accountant's CNP.
  - The company remains legally responsible even when the accountant's certificate is used.
  - [HappyWeb](https://happyweb.ro/index.php/articol/cum-delegi-accesul-in-spv-pentru-contabil-sau-angajat-ghid-pas-cu-pas-2026); [Notari.pro](https://notari.pro/procura/procura-semnatura-electronica-digitala-model-pret); [InfoTVA SPV documents](https://infotva.manager.ro/articole/infotva/documente-necesare-spv-persoane-juridice-24100.html)
- [TRADE] When a client relationship ends, SPV access is revoked with forms 151/153. — [InfoTVA](https://infotva.manager.ro/articole/infotva/contract-prestari-servicii-de-contabilitate-incheiat-cum-poate-fi-revocat-accesul-in-spv-23038.html)
- [VENDOR] ANAF accepts both USB-token and cloud-hosted certificates. With a cloud certificate, the provider hosts the key. — [ContApp](https://contapp.ro/blog/certificat-semnatura-digitala-ghid-complet/)
- [VENDOR blog] ANAF has announced a new portal and a new SPV with no launch date. How rights for multiple taxpayers will work hasn't been presented, and the article can't rule out that existing integrations will be affected. — [InsideApp](https://iapp.ro/articol/noul-portal-anaf-noul-spv-2026-functii-firme)
- [TRADE, mostly 2018–2019] GDPR for accounting firms:
  - A firm is the controller for its own staff and a processor (persoană împuternicită) for client data.
  - Art. 28 requires a written contract stating the subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of processing, data types and categories of people; the processor acts only on documented instructions, keeps staff bound by confidentiality, applies security measures and helps with data-subject requests.
  - Sub-processors need the controller's prior written authorisation, either specific or general; under a general one, changes must be notified so the client can object.
  - [Contabilul.ro](https://contabilul.manager.ro/a/23205/protectia-datelor-cu-caracter-personal-ce-obligatii-are-o-societate-de-contabilitate.html); [Contabilul.ro procedure](https://contabilul.manager.ro/a/23550/gdpr-pentru-firma-de-contabilitate-procedura-completa-pas-cu-pas.html); [CAFR good-practice guide](https://www.cafr.ro/uploads/GDPR_Cum_incepem_Ghid_bune_practici-9472.pdf); [GDPR text (RO)](https://gdpr-text.com/ro/?p=4155)

### Inferences
- Technically, a solo developer can reach production with the official API using existing open-source clients. The hard parts are operational:
  - looping per CUI within the per-CUI quotas;
  - downloading each message once and storing it, since there are 10 downloads per message per day and only a 60-day window;
  - refreshing tokens each year;
  - handling a possible migration to the "new SPV".
- Security and liability: a multi-client tool holds OAuth tokens that can unlock an accountant's whole client portfolio for up to a year. That calls for encryption at rest, access logs, revocation procedures and a DPA chain (client company → accounting firm → developer as sub-processor). Professional liability and cyber insurance are worth considering (inference; not researched).
- A chatbot or voice agent that handles patient or client data puts the developer in the same processor role for each client. Health data adds stricter requirements (inference; not sourced here).

### Inferences
- Credible price for a local solo developer: these are anchors, not evidence of what local buyers accept.
  - Voice receptionist for a single clinic: to compete with Callio and Vocalyy without their track record, the offer would have to sit at or below about €139–189/month, with a minute cap and per-minute overage. At $0.10–0.30/min, a 300–500-minute cap leaves a margin.
  - Text chatbot (website, WhatsApp, IG) done for the client: Sambla's 99–259 lei/month self-serve is the floor. A local done-for-you offer would need to justify a premium through setup, integration with booking, a webshop or WhatsApp, and ongoing tuning.
  - Accounting: standalone SPV tooling can hardly charge more than single-digit lei per client company per month. Workflow automation sold to one firm (n8n/OCR/reconciliation feeding Saga) is more plausibly a project fee plus a small retainer, but no Romanian evidence of accepted fees was found.
- Competition intensity: high in both. Voice for clinics is a crowded early market with funded or international players. Text chatbots compete with self-serve SaaS and CRM bundles. SPV download is saturated. Reconciliation and OCR are partly covered by suites (SmartBill Conta, FGO, Keez), and bespoke work around desktop Saga or WinMentor may be less served (unverified).
- Sales channels (inference):
  - clinics and salons: in-person local selling, plus integration partnerships with iStoma or MERO if their APIs allow;
  - accountants: the CECCAR county branch, the Contzilla community and accountants' Facebook groups (not verified), with a pilot at one or two local accounting firms as the cheapest test.
- Churn (inference): switching costs are low and monthly contracts common, so the developer must show measurable results (calls answered, bookings made, hours saved) every month. Platform cost changes (WhatsApp) and regulatory changes (ANAF) can wipe out margin or create unpaid maintenance.
- Fit with the constraints (inference): with under 5,000 lei of capital, usage-based infrastructure (numbers at $1–3/month, pay-per-minute or per-message APIs) is affordable. The binding constraint is the developer's time: 24/7 voice support expectations and monthly ANAF/e-Factura changes both eat into 10–15 h/week. Narrow, low-SLA offers (a text chatbot with booking hand-off; a single-firm automation pilot) fit better than broad SaaS.
