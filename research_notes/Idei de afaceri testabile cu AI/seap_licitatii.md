# Romanian public procurement (SEAP/SICAP, e-licitatie.ro): market for tender-monitoring and AI tender-document-analysis tools (research notes, state as of 8 Oct 2026)

*Method note: every fact below comes from web-search result snippets or summaries. No pages were fetched, per the tooling constraint, so spot-check figures on the cited page before relying on them. Source-type labels: [OFFICIAL] = government or EU/OECD body; [PRESS] = news media; [SPONSORED] = advertorial; [VENDOR] = the company's own site or marketing; [SECONDARY] = aggregator, third party or forum; [1-SRC] = found in only one source. ## 1. Market size and competition indicators

### Takeaway
SEAP/SICAP carries roughly 25,000–32,500 notice-based procedures a year, about 100–130 new procedures per working day. The awarded value of competitive procedures is roughly €19–25 bn a year. About 170,000 economic operators were registered as bidders around 2021, alongside more than 21,000 contracting authorities. Competition is weak: 44% of 2024 contract awards drew a single bid (scoreboard benchmark 28%), and 82% of procedures were decided on lowest price only. Direct-purchase volumes, active-bidder counts and average bids per tender were not found.

### Inferences
- **Daily flow is manageable:** 25,600–32,500 procedures a year over about 250 working days is about 100–130 new notice-based procedures per working day, plus direct-purchase notices. A solo developer can ingest and LLM-summarise every new notice each day at modest API cost. The bottleneck is getting the documents and running OCR, not volume.
- **Size the market on awarded value:** estimated launch values (446–669 bn lei a year) are 4–6 times awarded values (about 92–122 bn lei a year). The likely causes are framework-agreement maximum values, cancelled procedures and re-launches (inference). Use about €19–25 bn a year of awarded value for sizing.
- **Most procedures are SME-sized:** simplified (below-EU-threshold) procedures were about 63% of Q1 2024 launches and about 41% of 2023 awards.
- **Registered is not a prospect list:** about 170,000 registered bidders against about 26,000–35,000 competitive contracts awarded a year means most registrants win rarely or never.
- **Compliance matters more than proposal writing (untested):** a 44% single-bidder rate plus 82% lowest-price awards means evaluation is mostly price plus formal compliance. Eligibility and format-error checking is likely worth more to bidders than tools that write better technical proposals.
- **Volume is sensitive to public budgets:**
  - The 2025 dip of about 21% in count comes from a secondary source.
  - About 57% of 2024 value was EU-funded.
  - Volume is therefore exposed to austerity and to the end of PNRR. The PNRR deadline of Aug 2026 is background knowledge, not verified in this session.

## 2. Existing Romanian services and their prices

### Takeaway
The Romanian tender-alert market is crowded and cheap. At least 12 services publish prices, most between 15 and 300 RON a month. Several added LLM features in 2025–2026 that overlap the proposed product: AI eligibility scoring, AI summaries of technical documentation, AI "dossiers", AI search inside documents and AI-prepared bid files. No published fees were found for bid-preparation consultancy, success fees or DUAE help.

- **The core idea is already on the market.** CPV alerts, an AI summary of the caiet de sarcini and fișa de date, and an eligibility checklist are already advertised by at least SEAP Navigator AI, DataDriven (Expert), Tendro and Licitatia.ro, at 59–299 RON a month. Plain alerts are a commodity at 15–90 RON a month. A newcomer competing on features or price would face incumbents with SEO, apps, press presence and years of indexed data.
- **Credible price anchors for Romanian SMEs:**
  - about 40–100 RON a month for alerts;
  - about 130–300 RON a month for AI-assisted tiers;
  - €99–499 a month only for done-for-you bid-file preparation, from one vendor with unknown uptake.
- **Possible differentiators (not verified as unmet needs):**
  - bid-level Q&A over large scanned or signed PDFs, with OCR and citations to page and paragraph;
  - DUAE pre-fill and consistency checks against the fișa de date;
  - a human-checked compliance review sold per tender, not per month;
  - a workflow for consultants who prepare bids for many clients. SICAP.pro already sells multi-seat plans to consultants.
- **Acquisition channel:** competitors appear in sponsored or promotional press (ProTV advertorial, wall-street.ro, economica.net, spotmedia) and publish SEO guides. Content marketing and SEO is the established way customers are acquired.

## 4. Data access: open data, APIs, document availability, legal limits

### Takeaway
Notice and award metadata are accessible without scraping. data.gov.ro publishes yearly SEAP datasets as quarterly XLSX/CSV files, including direct purchases, under the OGL-ROU 1.0 open licence. e-licitatie.ro serves public notice pages and an undocumented "api-pub" JSON interface that several third parties already use. There is no documented official real-time API, and OCDS publication is incomplete. Two things were not verified: whether tender documents can be downloaded without an account, and what the portal's terms say about automated access.

### Cited Findings
**Official bulk data**
- **Yearly datasets:** data.gov.ro has one dataset per year (e.g., "Achiziții publice 2025") with quarterly XLSX files covering initiation notices, award notices, contracts, direct purchases and contract modifications. Earlier years are in XLSX or CSV, going back to 2007. [OFFICIAL] — [data.gov.ro 2025](https://data.gov.ro/dataset/achizitii-publice-2025); [2020](https://data.gov.ro/dataset/achizitii-publice-2020); [2017](https://data.gov.ro/dataset/achizitii-publice-2017); [2007–2016 contracts](https://data.gov.ro/dataset/achizitii-publice-2007-2016-contracte6); [catalogue search](https://data.gov.ro/dataset?q=achizitii+publice)
- **Licence:** data.gov.ro reuse is governed by the OGL ROU 1.0 licence, introduced in 2014 by the General Secretariat of the Government. [SECONDARY via search summary] — [CKAN ecosystem: data.gov.ro](https://ecosystem.ckan.org/site/data-gov-ro-data-gov-ro)
- **OCDS republication:** the GitHub project "achizitii-deschise" republishes SEAP data in OCDS format with comparable unit prices. It states that the quarterly exports cover 2016–2026 under OGL v1.0, and that Romania has not met its OCDS commitment for below-threshold, item-level data. It also notes that OpenTender, which is TED-based and licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial), is the only Romanian OCDS listing in the OCP registry. [SECONDARY] — [GitHub](https://github.com/CristianNichifor/achizitii-deschise)
- **OGP commitment RO0046 (Open Contracting):** planned OCDS adoption and a JSON web service. The end-of-term status suggests work had only just started. [OFFICIAL-intl] — [OGP RO0046](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/romania/commitments/RO0046/)

**Live and undocumented access**
- **api-pub endpoints:** a public Postman collection documents undocumented e-licitatie.ro "api-pub" endpoints: a POST call that lists notices and GET calls for entity records. Requests carry a Referer header pointing at the portal's notice list. The archive subdomain istoric.e-licitatie.ro serves JSON; a third-party Apify actor says the archive covers 2007–2018 and above-threshold tenders only. [SECONDARY] — [sicap-parser Postman collection](https://github.com/ciocan/sicap-parser/blob/master/data/postman-collection.json); [istoric.e-licitatie.ro](http://istoric.e-licitatie.ro/api-pub/C_PUBLIC_CANotice/get/123456)

- **Public notice pages:** e-licitatie.ro serves notices under /pub/ URLs, for example a contract notice and an award notice. [OFFICIAL pages appearing in search] — [contract notice](https://e-licitatie.ro/pub/notices/c-notice/v2/view/100196221); [award notice](https://e-licitatie.ro/pub/notices/ca-notices/view-c/100589450)

- **GDPR handling:** the Apify actor apnoda says it scrubs personal data "to satisfy GDPR" and links winners to awarded contracts. [VENDOR] — [Apify](https://apify.com/apnoda/seap-sicap-tenders)

**Documents**

### Inferences
- **No scraping needed for alerts and history,** but freshness depends on an undocumented API. data.gov.ro quarterly files cover history and market sizing; public notice pages and api-pub give fresh notices. - **Documents are obtainable but not easy:** competitors already summarise and OCR documentation, so third parties can clearly get the files. - **DUAE help may lose value later:** SEAP's growing links to ANAF and ONRC could reduce the need for DUAE help if registry data is pre-filled. This is speculative.

- **New platform:** its timeline, and whether api-pub will survive it, were not found.
- **Not pursued:** these items could not be researched before the search budget ran out.

## 5. Demand and pain points

### Takeaway
Evidence of pain is indirect. It consists of:
- weak competition (44% single-bidder);
- think-tank and press complaints about disproportionate bureaucracy;
- historically heavy litigation;
- a vendor founder's claim that qualification paperwork is the bottleneck for firms diversifying into public contracts.

Willingness to pay is shown mainly by the many paid alert services surviving at 15–300 RON a month and by Western VC funding. No survey of Romanian SMEs was found on SEAP pain points, on time cost, or on bids rejected for formal errors.

### Cited Findings
- **Low competition, price-driven awards:** 44% single-bidder awards in 2024 (benchmark 28%); 82% of procedures decided on lowest price. [OFFICIAL] — [Single Market Scoreboard](https://single-market-scoreboard.ec.europa.eu/node/612_ro)
- **Bureaucracy complaints:** a study by SAR (Societatea Academică din România) says bureaucratic measures imposed on procurement participants are "useless and disproportionate", mainly those set by the managing authorities of two EU-funded programmes. It adds that conflict-of-interest rules effectively exclude everyone at many small firms. The snippet is undated. [PRESS, HotNews] — [hotnews.ro](https://hotnews.ro/societatea-academica-din-romania-masuri-birocratice-inutile-si-disproportionate-impuse-participantilor-la-achizitiile-publice-742737)
- **Vendor founder's view (interested party, Mihai Negrea of DataDriven):**
  - smaller firms are shedding services, while mid-sized and larger firms turn to public tenders to diversify;
  - the paperwork needed to qualify is often the bottleneck;
  - AI makes filtering easier and can automate drafting, but it risks "amplifying paperwork instead of simplifying it".

  [PRESS interviews] — [Economedia video](https://economedia.ro/video-ai-in-licitatii-publice-cum-ajuta-cum-incurca-mihai-negrea-fost-it-ist-de-microsoft-si-fondator-de-platforma-care-monitorizeaza-licitatiile-publice-din-romania-pericolul-cu-inteligenta.html); [Economedia interview](https://economedia.ro/video-interviu-potentialul-licitatiilor-externe-pe-o-piata-internationala-mihai-negrea-datadriven-ro-la-fel-cum-firme-din-exterior-vin-si-ne-sufla-noua-contracte-in-romania-putem-sa-iesi.html)
- **Romanian firms rarely bid abroad:** Romanian-owned firms directly win fewer than about 100 public contracts a year in other EU countries, out of an EU market of about €670 bn a year, per DataDriven's analysis. [PRESS citing vendor data, 1-SRC] — [Economedia](https://economedia.ro/grafice-radiografie-a-pietei-mari-de-achizitii-publice-din-ue-analiza-sute-de-miliarde-de-euro-la-dispozitie-insa-firmele-romanesti-castiga-direct-sub-100-de-contracte-publice-pe-an.html)
- **Procurement as a hedge:** a Wall-Street.ro analysis calls public procurement an "anti-cyclical growth engine" while tax changes and rising energy and labour costs squeeze margins. The snippet is undated with no author and may be promotional. [PRESS] — [wall-street.ro](https://www.wall-street.ro/articol/fara-categorie/piata-de-achizitii-publice-un-motor-de-crestere-anti-ciclic)
- **Litigation history:**
  - "58% of all tenders last year were challenged" (economica.net, undated, historical). [PRESS] — [economica.net](https://www.economica.net/?p=115547)
  - CNSC found irregularities in procedures worth €4.9 bn in 2020. [PRESS] — [Economedia](https://economedia.ro/49-miliarde-de-euro-valoarea-procedurilor-de-achizitii-publice-in-care-cnsc-a-identificat-nereguli-in-2020.html)
- **Austerity:** an Economedia headline says public procurement is "quasi-blocked" by the "Austerity Ordinance". Title only, undated. [PRESS] — [Economedia](https://economedia.ro/?p=157861)
- **SME rules changed in 2016:** a Sept 2016 emergency ordinance removed the SME-specific 50% reductions in turnover and guarantee requirements after the EC treated them as positive discrimination. Buyers must now justify not dividing a contract into lots. [PRESS, 2016] — [economica.net](https://www.economica.net/?p=125610)
- **Willingness to pay at market level:** at least 12 Romanian paid alert/AI services publish prices (§2), and the category attracts Western venture funding (§3). [VENDOR/PRESS] — see sources in §2 and §3

### Inferences
- **Firms pay to find tenders; paying for analysis is unproven.** The strongest demand signal is that many paid alert services survive, some for 15–22+ years. Their low prices (typically about 70–90 RON a month for a mid-range plan) suggest the market values *finding* tenders cheaply. Whether firms will pay more for *analysis and compliance* is unproven.
- **Competitors are still testing AI pricing:** SEAP Navigator AI is in free beta and Tendro bundles only 1–15 AI reports a month.
- **Rejections are probably about price and form, not proposal quality.** With lowest price dominating, bids mostly fail on price or on formal compliance: DUAE, qualification documents, guarantees, deadlines. That points to an eligibility and compliance checker as the more defensible value. Direct evidence on rejection causes was not found.

### Gaps
- **No SME survey found:** nothing from CNIPMMR, Eurobarometer, the EC or others quantifying SME time cost, reasons for not bidding, or the share of Romanian bids rejected as inadmissible or non-compliant for formal errors.
- **No recent CNSC statistics:** annual figures for 2023–2025 were not found. A 2020 count (2,453 decisions, 596 appealed to courts) appeared in a search summary without a clear source page, so it is not used above.
- **No direct evidence of willingness to pay for AI document analysis:** no customer counts, testimonials or conversion rates.
- **Not tried before the search budget ran out:** "barometru IMM achiziții publice", "oferte inadmisibile", and Reddit or Facebook-group anecdotes.

## 6. Best first customer segments, reach channels and credible price points

### Takeaway
Evidence for choosing a segment is thin. What exists points to three groups:
- SMEs bidding in simplified procedures, which are the majority of procedure volume;
- consultants and agencies bidding for many clients, already a named tier at SICAP.pro;
- mid-sized firms diversifying into public contracts.

Competitors acquire customers mainly through SEO guides and paid or promotional press. Credible price points from the market are 40–100 RON a month for alerts and 130–300 RON a month for AI tiers. Higher prices appear only for done-for-you bid-file preparation (€99–499 a month, one vendor).

### Cited Findings
- **Simplified procedures dominate the count:**
  - 5,496 of about 8,746 procedures launched in Q1 2024. [OFFICIAL ANAP via SNAPER] — [snaper.ro](https://snaper.ro/2024/06/01/informare/)
  - In 2023 awards, simplified procedures were about 41% of contracts and 23% of value; open tenders about 34% and 66%. [OECD] — [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/managing-public-procurement-risks-in-romania_5dfda60e-en/full-report/applying-a-risk-management-approach-to-challenges-in-public-procurement_0eb67540.html)

- **Consultants are an existing tier:** SICAP.pro's top tier (2,000 RON a year, 5 seats, 50 alerts) is explicitly "for consultants and agencies"; its Start tier is for sole traders (PFAs) and small projects. [VENDOR] — [sicap.pro](https://sicap.pro/)

- **E-signature vendors reach every bidder:** qualified e-signature vendors such as AlfaSign publish SICAP enrolment guides, and every bidder needs a qualified e-signature. [VENDOR] — [alfasign.ro](https://alfasign.ro/cum-te-inrolezi-sicap-licitatii/)

### Inferences
- **Start with a service, not a subscription.** The alert market is a commodity, so the most testable entry for 10–15 hours a week and under 5,000 lei is a per-tender service, productised later. An example: a per-tender eligibility and document-risk report covering OCR of scanned annexes, a checklist of qualification requirements, DUAE points, deadlines and draft clarification questions, sold to a firm or consultant for a specific tender. This avoids competing head-on with 39–299 RON/month subscriptions. No data supports a price for such a report because consultancy fee benchmarks are missing, so the price must be tested.
- **Consultants are a plausible first segment:**
  - a competitor already sells multi-seat plans to them;
  - one consultant handles many tenders a month, so each account is worth more;
  - they carry the risk of formal errors.
- **Construction/PNRR works are probably the largest value pool,** given open tenders hold about 66% of awarded value and about 57% of value is EU-funded. Sector shares were not found.
- **Local channels to test (unverified):** for Bistrița and Nord-Vest, no data was found. Plausible channels are regional chambers of commerce, e-signature resellers and accounting firms.
- **Arithmetic check:** at about 100–150 RON a month net per subscriber, 35–50 paying customers bring in about 5,000 RON a month. With about 12 competitors at similar prices, reaching that needs a differentiated wedge or a services component.
